Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Digital Spawn ("we") operates an internal social media scheduling tool ("Unsealed History Postiz"). It is used only by the account owner to schedule and publish content to our own channels (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).

Information we access

When the owner connects an account, we receive an access token and basic profile and Page or channel information (name, ID, profile picture). We use this solely to publish and schedule the owner's own posts and to read basic post status.

How we use and store it

Tokens are stored on a private server controlled by the account owner. We do not sell, share or disclose this information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not collect data about other users or visitors.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. The owner can disconnect the account in the tool at any time, or revoke access in the platform settings, and the stored tokens are then deleted. See Data Deletion.
